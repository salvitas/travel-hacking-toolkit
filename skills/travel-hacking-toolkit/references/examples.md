# Prompt Examples

## Flight search
- Find the best flight options for SIN to Tokyo on 2026-05-10 to 2026-05-18. Rank by price, stops, and practicality.
- Search all relevant flight sources for JFK to London next March and tell me the best value option.

## Award search
- Find award availability for SFO to Tokyo in business class and tell me the best mileage play.
- Compare award options across programs for this route and show the math.

## Transfer partner optimization
- I have Chase UR and Amex MR. For this award price, which currency is cheapest to use?
- Map the award program to my transferable points and show the effective cost in each currency.

## Cash vs points
- Compare cash vs points for this trip and tell me whether I should pay cash or redeem points.
- Show the cents-per-point value and recommend the better option.
