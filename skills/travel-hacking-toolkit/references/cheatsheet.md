# Travel Hacking Cheat Sheet

## Flights
- Ask for route, dates, cabin, travelers, and flexibility.
- Search in parallel when possible.
- Prefer the best mix of price, routing, and reliability.

## Award searches
- Look up award availability first.
- Compare mileage cost across programs.
- Prefer the redemption with the lowest real cost and decent availability.

## Transfer partners
- Convert award cost with: `effective_points = award_miles / transfer_ratio`.
- Compare effective points across all transferable currencies.
- Rank by opportunity cost, not just nominal miles.

## Cash vs points
- Compare cash price vs effective point cost.
- Convert points to cash-equivalent value.
- Choose the option with better value unless flexibility or convenience changes the answer.

## Recommendation rule
- Be opinionated.
- Show the math.
- State the best option first.
- Mention tradeoffs only after the winner.
